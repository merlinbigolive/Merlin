No. Beginners are welcome to apply. Training, guidance, and support can be provided to help you get started.

How many hours do I need to be live every day? ＋

The required live hours may vary depending on your target and hosting schedule. Your agency team will guide you regarding the expected daily live hours.

Is there any joining fee? ＋

No. There is no joining fee. You can apply and join Merlin Agency without paying any joining charges.

What do I need to do while I'm live? ＋

While you're live, you can play PKs, interact and chat with your viewers, reply to comments, entertain your audience, and keep the live session engaging and active.
